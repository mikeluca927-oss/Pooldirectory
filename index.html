How to read this. Every property here has an in-ground pool permit confirmed in the town's own permit record, which is where the pool description comes from. Coverage is Wall, Marlboro and Asbury Park, the towns whose records could be checked online. Other Monmouth towns are not included because their permit descriptions are not public online. Town permit links need a free SDL Portal login. Owner names are withheld from the state parcel file, so mail goes to the mailing address on record. Stage and dates are as of November 17, 2026.
